# CloudFish Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11/16/2026_

CloudFish is a game made by Luke Morse ("we"). This policy explains what information the game and its advertising partner collect, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

We don't collect, store or sell any personal information ourselves. CloudFish has no accounts and no sign-in. Your settings and progress, such as mute and whether you've seen the tutorial, are saved only on your device.

## Advertising

CloudFish shows ads through **Google AdMob**. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

- your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
- your approximate location, based on your IP address
- information about the ads you see and tap, and how you use the game
- crash and performance information about the ads

Google's use of this data is described in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and on [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Your choices

- **Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland, and other regions where it's required):** the first time you open the game, you're asked whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time with the **Privacy** button on the pause screen.
- **iOS:** the game asks for permission to track before using your device's advertising identifier. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
- **Android:** you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

If you decline personalised ads, you still see ads, but they aren't based on your activity.

## Children

CloudFish is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
