I'm Luke. I write code and make noise.

  • Tap a letter to make a sound, drag to bend, shake to wobble, hold it down to blow it up. Tap the little balloon to swap sets, and the red dot to record a clip.
  • Or play your keyboard: rows 1–8, Q–I, A–K and Z–, are L, U, K and E, low to high. Got a MIDI keyboard?
  • Built with Flutter web. Sounds synthesized in Faust, compiled to Wasm.
